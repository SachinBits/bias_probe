Methods and limits

  • Ten T=0.7 repeats estimate response rates and noise; three T=0 repeats provide a stability check. With 10 repeats per variant, 10/10 vs 5/10 is detectable at p < 0.05, while 10/10 vs 7/10 is not.
  • Trap probes use one-sided Fisher exact tests; other probes use seeded permutation tests; Benjamini–Hochberg q-values correct across probes.
  • The fallback judge sees only the response text, allowed labels, and label help; it never sees the original question.
  • Refusals and hedges are not wrong; uncertainty labels such as unknown/unclear are content labels; unscorable is a scorer outcome.
  • The numbers do not show universal bias: they describe this model snapshot, wording, temperatures, English-only probes, and ten repeats.
